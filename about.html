About SANBS

Learn about the organisation and its role in blood transfusion services.

Who We Are

The South African National Blood Service (SANBS) is an independent non-profit organisation and a leader in blood transfusion services. It operates in eight of South Africa's nine provinces and connects voluntary donors with patients and healthcare facilities.

Its work includes collecting donated blood, processing it into useful components, testing products, and supporting hospitals with safe and reliable blood services.

Purpose
Trusted to save lives.
Vision
To be the cornerstone of healthcare services in South Africa through the gift of life.
Mission
To reliably provide trusted blood products and services to patients while maintaining appropriate levels of cost, efficiency and quality and innovating to improve patient outcomes.

Who We Serve

Donors

First-time and regular donors need clear eligibility, preparation and donation guidance.

Healthcare Professionals

Healthcare providers rely on accurate information about blood products and transfusion support.

Community Partners

Schools, businesses, churches and community organisations can help coordinate blood drives.
