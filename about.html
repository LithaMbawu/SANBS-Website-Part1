About SANBS

Learn about the organisation and its role in blood transfusion services.

Who We Are

The South African National Blood Service (SANBS) is an independent non-profit organisation and a leader in blood transfusion services. SANBS operates in eight of South Africa's nine provinces.

Purpose

Trusted to save lives.

Vision

To be the cornerstone of healthcare services in South Africa through the gift of life.

Mission

To reliably provide trusted blood products and services to patients while maintaining appropriate levels of cost, efficiency and quality and innovating to improve patient outcomes.

Target Audience

Donors

First-time and regular blood donors.

Healthcare Professionals

Healthcare providers who require blood and blood products.

Community Partners

Schools, businesses, churches and community organisations.
