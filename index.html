Support Options

Blood donation helps patients receiving treatment for cancer, surgery, trauma and chronic illness. Check your eligibility and book a donation visit.

Volunteers can support awareness campaigns, donor recruitment, community outreach and events that help SANBS reach more communities.

Schools, workplaces and community groups can host a blood drive to make blood donation accessible and encourage regular donors.

Why Blood Donation Matters

It saves lives

Each donation can help patients in surgery, trauma care, cancer treatment and maternity emergencies.

It strengthens care

Healthy blood stock supports hospitals and clinics with reliable, life-saving resources.

It brings communities together

Community drives and volunteer support make it easier for more people to give and care.
